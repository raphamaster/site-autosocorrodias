# Auto Socorro Dias — Design Language

## Direction
**Faixa de rodovia** — a practical emergency-response identity with contemporary editorial typography. It retains the client's recognized navy/red pairing. The visual impression should evoke reflective signage and disciplined roadside operations, never playful mascot art or a generic app dashboard.

## Typography
Barlow Condensed 800 for large compressed statements, DM Sans for paragraphs and controls. Remote Google Fonts with local sans fallback.

## Layout
Editorial split hero, distinctive hero image zone, compact trust band, readable service rows, three-step service progression, strong proof-of-experience section, direct-contact form, mobile sticky actions. Cards only when a concrete form needs a contained surface.

## Copy and proof
Action-first Brazilian Portuguese, direct and steady. Avoid false claims about travel times, payment acceptance, coverage, special equipment and customer reviews. 24h and 40+ years derive from client-provided material. Illustration is explicitly labeled.

## Interactions
WhatsApp call-to-action throughout; selection-specific prefilled requests; direct telephone link; responsive menu; accessible native FAQ details; form does not store data. Motion is restrained and obeys reduced motion.
